WEB SWINGER VR

VR: Push off ground/walls with your real hands to move & jump. Squeeze GRIP near a surface to grab & climb. Hold TRIGGER to shoot a web from that palm, release to let go. Move ONLY by pushing your controllers against the ground and walls. Stick up: reel web in, down: let out. Right stick left/right: turn. Click left stick: front flip, click right stick: back flip. A/X: shoot a web bolt (stuns NPCs). Swing-web an NPC to yank them to you. Release the trigger to let go. B/Y: menu.
Desktop: WASD, mouse look, Left/Right click = left/right web (hold), Space jump, Q/E web bolts, Shift reel, R release, F reset.
